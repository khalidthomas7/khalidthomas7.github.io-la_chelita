Our Menu

Handmade tortillas, family recipes, and flavors from Jalisco.

Breakfast Appetizers Salads Soups Sandwiches & Tortas Entrees Desserts Drinks

Breakfast

  • Chilaquiles Verde/Red Chelita's Recipe

    Tortilla chips, salsa, fried eggs, queso fresco, avocado, and crema.

  • Breakfast Tacos

    Scrambled eggs, chorizo, cheese, and potatoes on fresh handmade corn tortillas.

  • Huevos Rancheros

    Fried eggs on tortillas, smothered in salsa ranchera, served with refried beans.

  • Migas

    Scrambled eggs with crispy tortilla strips, onions, tomatoes, peppers, and cheese.

  • Molletes

    Toasted bolillo with refried beans, melted cheese, and pico de gallo.

  • Tamales de Desayuno

    Breakfast tamales (rajas con queso or bean and cheese), served with salsa verde.

  • Avocado Toast Tejano

    Toasted bolillo, smashed avocado, queso fresco, pickled jalapeños, and pico de gallo.

Appetizers

  • Elote (Street Corn)

    Grilled corn with mayonesa, cotija cheese, lime, and chile piquín.

  • Nachos Loaded

    House-fried chips with melted cheese, black beans, jalapeños, crema, and guacamole.

  • Queso Fundido

    Melted Chihuahua cheese with chorizo, served with fresh tortillas.

  • Quesadillas

    Grilled tortillas with cheese and choice of chicken tinga, carne asada, or vegetables.

  • Jalapeño Rellenos

    Jalapeños stuffed with cheese, coated in egg batter, and fried.

Salads

  • Ensalada de Nopales

    Grilled cactus, tomatoes, onions, queso fresco, and cilantro with lime vinaigrette.

  • Taco Salad

    Seasoned ground beef or chicken, lettuce, cheese, tomatoes, and guacamole in a crispy tortilla bowl.

  • Grilled Chicken Salad

    Mixed greens, avocado, black beans, corn, tomatoes, and crispy tortilla strips with chipotle ranch.

Soups

  • Caldo de Pollo

    Hearty chicken soup with vegetables, rice, and fresh lime.

  • Chicken Tortilla Soup

    Tomato broth with shredded chicken, crispy tortilla strips, avocado, and cheese.

  • Pozole Rojo Saturday Special

    Traditional hominy and pork soup with red chile broth.

  • Frijoles Charros

    Pinto bean soup with bacon, chorizo, and vegetables.

Sandwiches & Tortas

Add papas fritas or ensalada.

  • Torta de Carne Asada

    Grilled steak, avocado, lettuce, tomato, beans, and jalapeños on telera bread.

  • Torta de Milanesa

    Breaded chicken or beef, lettuce, tomato, avocado, and beans on telera.

  • Chorizo Burger

    Half beef, half chorizo patty with grilled onions, queso, and jalapeños.

  • Torta Ahogada Chelita's Specialty

    Carnitas torta "drowned" in spicy tomato salsa (Guadalajara-style).

Entrees

  • Birria de Res Chelita's Recipe

    Slow-braised beef in Jalisco-style chile broth, served with consommé, tortillas, onions, and cilantro.

  • Carne Asada Plate

    Grilled marinated steak with rice, charro beans, grilled onions, and tortillas.

  • Enchiladas

    Rolled tortillas with chicken, beef, or cheese in red or green sauce, served with rice and beans.

  • Tacos de Pescado

    Grilled fish with cabbage slaw, pico de gallo, and chipotle crema on corn tortillas.

  • Chiles Rellenos

    Roasted poblano peppers stuffed with cheese or picadillo, topped with salsa roja.

  • Fajitas

    Sizzling chicken, steak, or shrimp with peppers and onions, served with guacamole, crema, and tortillas.

  • Tamales Plate

    Two handmade tamales (pork in red chile, chicken in verde, or rajas con queso) with rice and beans.

  • Carne en su Jugo Weekend Special

    Jalisco-style beef stew with beans, bacon, and tomatillo broth.

  • Tex-Mex Combination

    Beef taco, cheese enchilada, and tamale with rice and beans.

Desserts

  • Churros con Chocolate

    Cinnamon-sugar churros with Mexican chocolate dipping sauce.

  • Flan de Cajeta

    Creamy caramel custard made with goat's milk caramel.

  • Pastel de Tres Leches

    Light sponge cake soaked in three milks, topped with whipped cream.

  • Buñuelos

    Crispy fried dough dusted with cinnamon sugar.

  • Arroz con Leche

    Creamy rice pudding with cinnamon and raisins.
